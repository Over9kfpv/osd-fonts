How they're made

Only letters, digits and punctuation are replaced. Every OSD symbol (battery, RSSI, horizon, arrows, logo) comes from an icon set. Fonts with 8 px letters are doubled vertically; taller EGA and VGA fonts are used as they are. Every glyph gets a 1 px black outline so it stays readable over video.

Flash a font

Download the .mcm and upload it with Betaflight Configurator's Font Manager, or upload straight from the Mix page or the editor in Chrome or Edge. See Install.
