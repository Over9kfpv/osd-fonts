How these fonts are made

Letters from the retro font

Only letters, digits and punctuation are replaced. Every OSD symbol (battery, RSSI, horizon, arrows, logo) comes from an icon set, so nothing on your OSD breaks.

Readable over video

Letters are centred at 1:1 in the 12×18 cell, or doubled in height if you pick Tall. Every glyph gets a 1 px black outline, like the stock Betaflight font.

Flash it

Download the .mcm and upload it with Betaflight Configurator's Font Manager, or upload it straight from the Mix page or the editor in Chrome or Edge. See Install.
